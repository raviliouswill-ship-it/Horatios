The Story

A Nod to the Admiral

Horatio Nelson was born a few miles from here, in Burnham Thorpe, and Norfolk has never quite let him go. Horatio's plays on that in the friendliest possible way: a Full Nelson on the breakfast menu, a Half Nelson for smaller appetites, and a genuinely enormous fruit scone that regulars still bring up years later. Run by Martin and Sarah, just off Holt's main street on Bull Street, it has been quietly one of the town's most popular breakfast spots for well over a decade.

The kitchen leans hearty and traditional: cottage pie, steak and kidney pudding, a beef and mushroom ale pie that regulars ask for by name. Afternoon tea needs no booking, arriving on a proper three-tier stand, made fresh while it is waited for. In summer, a Mr Whippy machine appears outside — reportedly the first in Holt to do so — for anyone after something colder.

"A birthday nobody mentioned, and a cake box sent home anyway." A visitor, via review
